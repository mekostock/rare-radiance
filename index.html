How to Order

Rare Radiance is an online store — there’s no checkout on this page. Ordering is personal, by message, in three simple steps.

01

Browse & Pick a Category

Look through rings, necklaces, earrings, bracelets and custom pieces above, and note the style you have in mind.

02

Send a Message

Use the order form below to build your message, copy it, and send it as a DM. Name the piece you saw, or ask what else is available in your category.

03

Confirm Your Piece

Confirm the piece, price and delivery details directly in your message conversation, then complete your order there.
